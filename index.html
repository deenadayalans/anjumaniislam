What we noticed on the reference schools

Common homepage pattern

  • Hero banner with 3–5 rotating messages
  • Founder or chairperson quote
  • Programmes by age or curriculum
  • Campus and virtual tour
  • News, achievements, results
  • Persistent admissions CTA

Where we can do better

  • A single “Apply” flow with a downloadable form and an online enquiry
  • Fees policy and enrolment steps visible, not buried in a PDF
  • Live calendar rather than a static list
  • WhatsApp and call buttons for parents
  • Fast, mobile-first pages

Our unique selling points

  • 174-acre hill campus in Panchgani
  • Robotics, aviation, agriculture, horse riding, archery
  • On-campus NEET, JEE, CET, NATA, CA coaching
  • 100+ years of heritage inside the 150-year Anjuman network

Build options

Recommended

A. WordPress (or similar CMS)

Staff can edit news, calendar and admissions text without a developer. Large ecosystem, easy hosting.

  • Fast to launch, low running cost
  • Needs regular updates and security care

B. Static site (Astro / Next.js) + headless CMS

Very fast and secure, with the same editing convenience.

  • Best performance and SEO
  • Higher initial development effort

C. Site builder (Wix / Squarespace / Webflow)

Quickest to launch with no hosting to manage.

  • Limited customisation for forms and portals
  • Monthly platform fee

Gaps and inconsistencies in the prospectus

  • Calendar dates run from 2024–25 and need to be refreshed for 2026–27.
  • “Dr. Zahir Kazi” and “Dr. Zaheer Kazi” are both used; the spelling needs confirming.
  • Section 8 mentions “Horizon’s unique approach”, which looks like copied text from another school.
  • Email domains differ (anjumanislampublicschool.in vs anjumanislampublicschoolpanchgani.in); one official domain is needed.
  • The prospectus has no logo, photographs, fee amounts, board affiliation (SSC/HSC assumed), class range or co-ed status. These are needed before build.
  • The admission form page is blank.
  • Statements such as “world-class facilities” and “below-average performance will not be entertained” should be reviewed before publishing.
