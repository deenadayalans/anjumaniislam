Our Heritage

From 1874 to today

Part of Anjuman-I-Islam, founded by Justice Badruddin Tyabji — now 97 institutions serving over one lakh students.

1874
Anjuman-I-Islam founded in Mumbai “to see the Muslim community advance in education and social standing.”
2018–19
Golden School Award by Indian Talent Search.
2020
Centenary celebration — 100 successful years of education and leaders across the globe.
2024
Robotics, Agriculture and Basic Aviation added alongside horse riding, pottery and archery.
