Our story · since 1874
It began with one school in Bombay, founded by Justice Badruddin Tyabji.

Tyabji was the third President of the Indian National Congress and the first Indian to serve as Acting Chief Justice of the Bombay High Court. For more than a hundred years, Panchgani has been part of that promise.
