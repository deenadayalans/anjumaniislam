To cultivate a dynamic learning community within our boarding school where every student is empowered to explore, discover and excel — fostering academic excellence, character development and global citizenship, and inspiring a lifelong love of learning.

Our Story

Over a century in Panchgani

With more than 100 years of quality education, the Panchgani campus is home to 174 acres of flora and fauna.

1874

Anjuman-I-Islam founded in Mumbai by Justice Badruddin Tyabji and a group of devout, progressive Muslims.

2018–19

Golden School Award by Indian Talent Search.

2020

Centenary celebration — 100 successful years of education and leaders across the globe.

2024

Robotics, Agriculture and Basic Aviation added alongside horse riding, pottery and archery.

Leadership

Dr. Zahir Kazi, Padma Shri

President, Anjuman-I-Islam

A distinguished educationist and recipient of the Padma Shri, India’s fourth-highest civilian honour. Born in Ponda, Goa, he is a prominent figure in the educational and social welfare sectors.

Under his leadership Anjuman-I-Islam has expanded its reach, running around 97 schools, colleges, hostels, orphanages and other institutions across Maharashtra that cater to over one lakh students, primarily from minority communities. Despite his success as a radiologist, he reduced his medical practice to devote more time to philanthropic work.

The Panchgani Campus

174 acres of hills, flora and fauna

At the heart of the campus is a vintage academic block with projectors, science and computer labs and a library of books and digital resources. Beyond it: a performing arts centre with music rooms, a sports complex with indoor and outdoor courts and a gymnasium, renovated dormitories and a spacious dining hall.

Sustainability is part of campus life — rainwater harvesting and organic gardens reduce our footprint and teach the value of conservation.
