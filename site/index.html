All FAQs
Is this a boarding-only school?
It is a residential boarding school and junior college. Facilities such as dormitories, a dining hall for 300 and a 24/7 sick bay are on campus.
Is coaching for NEET, JEE, CET, NATA and CA included?
Coaching is offered on campus. Competitive courses attract extra fees.
What is the admission process?
Parents’ interview, child’s interview, written exam, medical history and blood investigation (Std 8 onwards).
Is there a prayer hall?
Yes. The call to prayer is given five times a day and Muslim students are expected to offer salah. Basic Islamic education and Quran recitation are emphasised.
