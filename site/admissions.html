Important points

  • Admission is subject to performance in the written exam.
  • Students with an addiction history will not be admitted.
  • An undertaking / indemnity on a ₹500 bond is mandatory.
  • Over-age students will not be enrolled.
  • Strict disciplinary action is taken against those who break the rules.

Fees policy

  • Fees are collected in two terms.
  • The first instalment is paid once admission is confirmed.
  • The second instalment is paid in the second semester, after the Diwali holiday.
  • For the balance, parents submit a post-dated cheque along with a promissory note.
  • Competitive coaching courses attract extra fees.

Is it a boys’ or girls’ school?
The school provides sports coaching and facilities for both boys and girls. Details of dormitory arrangements will be added by the school.
When does the academic session begin?
In the sample calendar, the session begins on 20 May. Dates are tentative; please confirm with the school office before booking travel.
Are competitive exam courses included in the fees?
No. Coaching for NEET, JEE, CET, NATA and CA attracts extra fees.
How are fees paid?
In two terms: the first after admission is confirmed and the second after the Diwali holiday. A post-dated cheque and promissory note secure the balance.
What medical care is available?
A trained nurse is on campus 24/7, a doctor visits for check-ups and the sick bay can keep patients in-house.
Is religious practice compulsory?
Prayer is called five times a day and Muslim students are expected to offer salah. Basic Islamic education and Quran recitation are emphasised.
Are there extra supports for weaker students?
Yes — peer tutors, resident advisors, academic advisors and extra coaching for vernacular-medium students who are especially weak in studies.
