Boarding & Care

Everything a boarder needs, on campus

Stay

Dormitories

All dormitories have undergone complete renovation — clean, hygienic and spacious, with common areas to relax and socialise, supervised by dorm in-charges.

Eat

Dining hall & food

A renovated hall for 300 diners serving five meals a day. The kitchen is carefully supervised and menus are designed by a dietician.

Faith

Prayer hall

The call to prayer is given five times a day and Muslim students are expected to offer salah. Basic Islamic education and Quran recitation are emphasised.

Health

Sick bay & infirmary

A trained nurse is on campus 24/7, a doctor visits for check-ups, and the sick bay can keep patients in-house.

Safety

Safe campus

Administrative staff ensure the campus is safe and secure, with strict disciplinary standards for everyone.

Green

Sustainable living

Rainwater harvesting and organic gardens teach conservation as part of everyday life.
