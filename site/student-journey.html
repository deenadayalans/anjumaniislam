How our students evolve

For many, the school year is a transformative experience filled with challenges, triumphs and invaluable lessons. This is the path a student takes with us.

Stage 1

Arrive & settle

Orientation for new students, a warm welcome from the dorm in-charge and the first friendships.

  • Welcome assembly and orientation
  • Dorm in-charge as first point of contact
Stage 2

Build foundations

Study habits, reading and core subjects, with peer tutors and resident advisors beside them.

  • Supervised study sessions
  • Unit tests and regular advising
Stage 3

Explore passions

Robotics, riding, archery, debate, pottery, sport — students try, fail, improve and find what they love.

  • Inter-house competitions
  • Clubs, leadership and community service
Stage 4

Rise to the board year

Preliminary exams, practicals and one-to-one goal setting for Std X and XII.

  • Prelim exams and practicals
  • Data-driven interventions where needed
Stage 5

Launch

Entrance exams, college admissions and a farewell at the Annual Day — carrying values and friendships for life.

  • NEET, JEE, CET, NATA and CA guidance
  • Prize distribution and farewell
